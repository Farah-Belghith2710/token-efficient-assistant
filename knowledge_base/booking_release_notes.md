# KestrelBook 3.2: release notes
Source: Release notes (booking system team) - released 6 Oct 2026

## New
- Recurring bookings: you can now repeat a booking every week, for up to 8 weeks.
- Reminder e-mails are now sent 2 hours before a booking starts (before: 30 minutes).

## Fixed
- Two people could sometimes book the same cleanroom bay at the same time. This is fixed.

## Known issues
- New bookings can take up to 15 minutes to appear in your Outlook calendar. The booking is valid immediately; only the calendar sync is slow.
