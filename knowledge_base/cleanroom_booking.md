# Booking the cleanroom
Source: Facilities wiki (cleanroom team) - last edited 2 Oct 2026

## Before your first booking
Only people who passed the cleanroom gowning training (course CR-101) can book the cleanroom. The training takes half a day and is valid for 12 months. You can register for CR-101 in the training catalogue on the intranet.

## How to book
Bookings are made in KestrelBook: choose "Cleanroom", then pick one of the four bays (Bay 1 to Bay 4). Every booking must be made at least 48 hours in advance, and one slot can last at most 4 hours. Bay 4 is reserved for lithography work.

## Cancelling and no-shows
Cancel at least 24 hours before your slot so that someone else can use it. A booking that is not cancelled and not used counts as a no-show. After three no-shows in one semester, your booking rights are suspended for 30 days.

## Contact
