# Meeting rooms and the auditorium
Source: Facilities wiki - last edited 12 Sep 2026

## Normal meeting rooms
Meeting rooms are booked in the Outlook calendar like a person: just add the room to your invitation. Rooms with a "VC" label have a camera and a speaker. Press "Join" on the touch panel to start the call.

## The auditorium
The auditorium in building B has 120 seats. It cannot be booked directly: send a request to the facilities desk at least 5 working days before your event.

## Catering
Coffee and lunch for meetings are ordered through the facilities desk on extension 4500. Orders must be placed before 14:00 on the working day before the meeting.
