# Research data storage policy
Source: Policy repository (Research Office) - version 3.1, June 2026

## Where data must be stored
Project data belongs on the project share (\\files\projects\project-name). Your personal drive (H:) is only for drafts and personal notes. Never keep the only copy of research data on a laptop or a USB stick.

## Backups
Project shares and home drives are backed up every night at 02:00. Backups are kept for 90 days. To restore a deleted file, open a ticket with the IT helpdesk and give the path and the date.

## How long data is kept
Research data must be kept for at least 10 years after the end of the project, together with a short README file that explains the data.

## Confidential data and sharing
Confidential data must be stored encrypted and must never be copied to USB sticks. To share files with external partners, use KestrelShare: its links expire after at most 14 days. Do not use personal cloud accounts.
