# Business travel and expenses
Source: Finance policy repository - version 2026

## Before you travel
Every business trip must be approved by your team lead before anything is booked. Trains, flights and hotels are booked through TravelDesk, the travel portal of the lab.

## Choosing transport
For destinations under 500 km, take the train when possible. Flights shorter than 6 hours are booked in economy class.

## Allowances and expenses
When you travel abroad, you receive a daily meal allowance of 45 euros. Submit your expense report in TravelDesk within 30 days after you return, with photos of all receipts.
