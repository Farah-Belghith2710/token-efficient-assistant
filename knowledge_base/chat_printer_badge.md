## Lena: my new badge does not release print jobs (27 Oct)
Lena: Since Monday my badge does not release my print jobs on printer PRN-3F-01. It just beeps. Is the printer broken?
Facilities desk: The printer is fine. Everyone received a new badge last week, and a new badge has to be registered once before it works with the printers. Tap your new badge on any printer, choose "Register badge" and log in with your lab username. Print jobs stay in the queue for 24 hours, after that they are deleted.
