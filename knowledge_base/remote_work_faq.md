# Working from home: FAQ
Source: HR FAQ - last edited 22 Aug 2026

## How often can I work from home?
You can work from home up to 2 days per week, if your team lead agrees. Register your home-working days in MyHR.

## What do I need at home?
All internal systems are only available through the VPN. New employees can use a one-time home-office budget of 300 euros for a chair, a screen or a headset.

## Can I work from another country?
Working from another country, even for a few days, needs written approval from HR in advance, because of tax and insurance rules.
