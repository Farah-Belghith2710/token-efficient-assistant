# New joiner checklist
Source: HR onboarding page - last edited 1 Sep 2026

## Your first day
Pick up your badge at the reception in building A, from 08:30. Bring an identity document. Your team lead or your buddy will meet you there.

## Accounts and equipment
Your lab account is created within 2 working days after HR has registered you. Until then you can use the guest Wi-Fi. Your laptop is prepared by IT before you arrive.

## Mandatory training
Everyone must follow the general safety training within the first 5 working days. You are not allowed to enter any laboratory before you have completed it. Lab-specific trainings, such as cleanroom gowning, come afterwards.

## Your buddy
Each new joiner gets a buddy from the same team for the first three months. Your team lead chooses the buddy.
