# Personal protective equipment in the wet lab
Source: Safety wiki (EHS team) - last edited 18 Sep 2026

## Always required
In the wet lab, always wear safety glasses, a lab coat, closed shoes and long trousers. Contact lenses are only allowed together with closed safety goggles.

## Gloves
Use nitrile gloves for most work, and change them as soon as they are contaminated. Never touch door handles, keyboards or phones with gloves on.

## Lab coats
Lab coats are washed by the laundry service. Put used coats in the blue bin at the entrance: the bin is collected every Friday.
