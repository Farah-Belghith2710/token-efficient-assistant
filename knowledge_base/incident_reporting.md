# Reporting a safety incident or near miss
Source: Safety procedures (EHS team) - procedure EHS-07, revision 4

## In an emergency
Call the internal emergency number 5555 from any lab phone. From a mobile phone, call 112 and then inform the reception. Stay with the injured person if it is safe to do so.

## Chemical spills
Warn the people around you and leave the area if the substance is unknown. For small spills of known chemicals, use the spill kit in the red cabinet next to each wet lab exit. Never clean up an unknown chemical alone.

## Reporting
Every incident and every near miss must be reported in SafeLog within 24 hours. A near miss is an event where nobody was hurt but someone could have been, for example a bottle that fell without breaking. Your supervisor automatically receives a copy of the report.
