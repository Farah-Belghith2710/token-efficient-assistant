# IT helpdesk: frequently asked questions
Source: IT service portal FAQ - last edited 5 Oct 2026

## How do I contact the helpdesk?
Call extension 4412, send an e-mail to helpdesk@kestrel-lab.example, or open a ticket in the IT portal. The helpdesk is staffed Monday to Friday from 08:00 to 17:30.

## How do I reset my password?
Use the self-service page password.kestrel-lab.example. You need your phone with the Kestrel Authenticator app. Passwords expire every 180 days and must have at least 14 characters.

## How fast will my ticket be handled?
- P1 (a whole team cannot work): first response within 1 hour.
- P2 (one person cannot work): first response within 4 working hours.
- P3 (questions and small problems): first response within 2 working days.

## How do I get a new laptop?
Ask your team lead to approve a request in the IT portal. Standard laptops are delivered in about 5 working days.
